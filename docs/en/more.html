# Further method catalogues

We hope that this User Experience method catalogue has provided you with useful ideas and inspiration for your work. If you are looking for even more method descriptions, then take a look at our following websites.

# Design Thinking Methods Catalogue

If you are interested in a human-centered approach to problem-solving and generating innovative ideas, you can find recommended methods in our Design Thinking Method Catalogue (opens new window). The methods are organized according to the phases of the design thinking process and each includes an estimate of the time required and the level of experience needed.
